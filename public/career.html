Career & Employability

Education, practical skills, internships, and employment — connected. Here's how SKC supports your career, not just your certificate.

Career Services

Support Beyond the Classroom

SKC positions itself as a career and skills institution — not just a college with courses. That means practical support connecting education to employment.

CV & Cover Letter Preparation

Practical workshops on building a professional CV and cover letter tailored to local employers.

Interview Preparation

Mock interviews and coaching so graduates walk into real interviews with confidence.

Job Search Skills

Guidance on where and how to find real openings, apply effectively, and follow up professionally.

Career Counseling

One-on-one guidance to help students choose the right School, program, and career direction.

Internship Placement

Support connecting learners with practical attachment opportunities as SKC's partnerships grow.

Entrepreneurship Support

Basic guidance on starting a small business or freelance practice for graduates who want to work for themselves.

Where Graduates Can Work

Career Pathways by School

SKC is a founding institution — these are the sectors and employer types each School is designed to prepare learners for.

Information Technology

NGOs and international organizations, telecom companies, banks and financial institutions, government offices, private IT service companies, schools, and small businesses needing digital support.

Engineering & Technical Studies

Construction and contracting firms, electrical and solar installation businesses, water and sanitation projects, and self-employment as a skilled tradesperson.

Social & Human Services

NGOs and community organizations, government offices, front-desk and administrative roles in private companies, and community development programs.

Health Skills

Community health outreach programs, NGOs delivering health and WASH projects, schools and workplaces needing first aid capacity, and local health support roles.

English & Communication

Any workplace requiring professional English communication — office roles, customer-facing positions, and further academic study.
